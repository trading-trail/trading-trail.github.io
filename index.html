How each video is made

  1. Filed

    Insiders report trades to the SEC, usually within two business days. Filings are public on SEC EDGAR.

  2. Checked

    Routine activity like scheduled grants and tax withholding is filtered out. Every number in a script is checked against the original filing.

  3. Explained

    The trade is shown on a price chart with plain-language narration, and every video links to its source filing.

Not financial advice. Videos describe legally reported transactions for information only. They don't recommend buying or selling any security, and a reported trade doesn't imply wrongdoing. Always check the original filing on SEC EDGAR.
